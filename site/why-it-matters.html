Why it matters

Open space is essential infrastructure

Open space is far more than a place for recreation — it is essential infrastructure that supports the health, liveability and resilience of our cities.

As populations grow and urban density increases, access to high-quality public open space becomes increasingly important.

Creating stronger communities
Vibrant places made for people
Squares, pocket parks and pedestrian-friendly streets bring communities to life, hosting markets, festivals, performances and everyday social interaction.
Healthier by design
Parks, plazas and green spaces encourage outdoor activity, improving mental health, reducing stress and fostering a stronger sense of belonging.
Thriving local businesses
Attractive public spaces encourage people to stay longer, increasing foot traffic and supporting cafés, restaurants and local retailers.
Cooler, greener streets
Trees and landscaped open spaces provide shade, reduce urban heat and create more comfortable environments.
Why it matters for growing centres

As Crows Nest continues to grow, open space becomes even more important.

Higher-density living often means residents have limited private outdoor space. Parks, plazas and pedestrian-friendly streets become the community's shared backyard — places to relax, exercise, meet friends and enjoy everyday life.

Well-designed public spaces also strengthen local centres. They attract people, support local businesses, enhance nearby property values, and create vibrant neighbourhoods that thrive socially and economically.

Open space is essential infrastructure

Successful cities understand that great public spaces are not a luxury — they are essential infrastructure.

Just like transport, housing and utilities, parks and pedestrian-friendly streets are critical to creating healthy, resilient and prosperous communities. They support  wellbeing, strengthen local economies, foster social connection and  cities adapt to a changing climate.

Investing in quality public space is an investment in a more liveable, sustainable future.
